Machinus privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Leer en español

In short: Machinus collects no personal data. It works offline and only keeps your saved games and settings on your own device.

Machinus is an Android game by Adrian Duardo. This policy explains what data the game handles. It is short because the game collects no personal data.

What data the game collects

None. Machinus:

  • requests no device permissions;
  • never connects to the internet or sends anything to any server, ours or a third party’s;
  • has no advertising, analytics, usage statistics or tracking;
  • uses no device or advertising identifiers;
  • has no accounts, sign-in or text fields;
  • has no in-app purchases.

What it stores on your device

So you can continue playing, the game stores, in the app’s private storage and only on your device:

  • your saved games (up to three): where you are, what you have solved, what you carry and the time played;
  • your settings: volume, accessibility and display options.

These files never leave your device, are excluded from cloud backup and are deleted when you uninstall the game. You can delete a saved game from within the game.

Google Play

Purchase and download happen through Google Play, which handles its own data under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal data about buyers from Google, only the aggregated sales reports Google provides to developers.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

This website

This website is static: it uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party resources, and it has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as your IP address for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, we will update this page and the date above before releasing it.
